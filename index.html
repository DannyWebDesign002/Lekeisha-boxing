Behind The Gloves

"I don't box for the money — I box because I love it. I love what I do and I am so grateful to be living my dream life representing Australia on the world stage."
— Lekeisha Pergoliti

I'm one of Australia's most exciting boxing talents — a 23-year-old from the small southwestern town of Australind, Western Australia, who has risen to the very top of the international amateur game.

Born to first-generation Italian-Australian parents on 20 April 2002, I grew up surrounded by sport. My father Vince built a backyard boxing gym, but it was soccer and basketball that captured my heart first — until a broken ankle at 14 changed everything. I stepped into the ring and never looked back.

My first amateur bout, fought as a 14-year-old against a more experienced opponent, ended in a unanimous decision win. That was the first of many. Today, more than 50 amateur bouts later, I carry a 2025 World Boxing Championship Silver Medal, two National titles, three State titles, and a Bronze Medal from my Commonwealth Games debut for Australia in Glasgow, 2026.

2016
My first amateur bout — won by unanimous decision at just 14 years old.
2018
Won State, National, and National Golden Gloves titles at junior and youth level.
2019
Repeated the Golden Gloves feat; claimed the Australian Women's Welterweight title by unanimous decision.
2022
Won the WA State title at Elite Senior level — a three-peat across junior, youth and senior.
2023
Awarded my Sport Australia Hall of Fame Tier 2 Scholarship.
2024
Claimed the Australian National Championship; won my first international gold at the Independence Cup, Dominican Republic.
2025
World Boxing Championship Silver Medal in Liverpool. Named CGA Emerging Athlete of the Month (September).
2026
Selected for the Australian team, I won Bronze on my Commonwealth Games debut in Glasgow — defeating Blessing Orakwe (NGA) before falling to Chantelle Reid (GBR). The journey continues...

// Security headers (.htaccess)
Header set X-Frame-Options "SAMEORIGIN"
Header set X-Content-Type-Options "nosniff"
Header set Referrer-Policy "strict-origin-when-cross-origin"
Header set Permissions-Policy "camera=(), microphone=()"
Header always set Strict-Transport-Security "max-age=31536000"
RewriteEngine On
RewriteCond %{HTTPS} off
RewriteRule ^ https://%{HTTP_HOST}%{REQUEST_URI} [L,R=301]
Contact form backend (contact.php): Create a PHP file that validates POST data, applies rate limiting by IP, sanitises all inputs, and sends email via PHPMailer. Never expose API keys in frontend code. Use environment variables for SMTP credentials.
Hosting recommendation: SiteGround or WP Engine (Australia region) for Australian SEO advantage. Enable Cloudflare CDN for performance and DDoS protection. Use Let's Encrypt for free SSL.
