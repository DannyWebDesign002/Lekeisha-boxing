I'm one of Australia's most exciting boxing talents — a 23-year-old from the small southwestern town of Australind, Western Australia, who has risen to the very top of the international amateur game.

Born to first-generation Italian-Australian parents on 20 April 2002, I grew up surrounded by sport. My father Vince built a backyard boxing gym, but it was soccer and basketball that captured my heart first — until a broken ankle at 14 changed everything. I stepped into the ring and never looked back.

My first amateur bout, fought as a 14-year-old against a more experienced opponent, ended in a unanimous decision win. That was the first of many. Today, more than 46 amateur bouts later, I carry a 2025 World Boxing Championship Silver Medal, two National titles, three State titles, and a coveted spot on the Australian Commonwealth Games team for Glasgow 2026.

2016
My first amateur bout — won by unanimous decision at just 14 years old.
2018
Won State, National, and National Golden Gloves titles at junior and youth level.

2025
World Boxing Championship Silver Medal in Liverpool. Named CGA Emerging Athlete of the Month (September).
2026
Selected for the Australian team — my Commonwealth Games debut in Glasgow. The journey continues...

📋 Edit Fight Record

How to edit fight record: Open index.html in a text editor, find the FIGHT_RECORD array in the JavaScript section near the bottom. Add or edit entries following the same format. Each entry has: date, opponent, country, result, method, event, location, weight.
// Example fight entry:

Edit Achievements

How to update achievements: Find the achievements section in the HTML. Update the .achievement-card divs directly. The three featured cards at the top use .achievement-feature — the gold card is the primary achievement. For new achievements, copy an existing card and update the icon, title, event, and badge.

📰 Post News Articles

How to add news: Find the #news section. Copy an existing <article class="news-card"> block and update the tag, title, excerpt, and date. For the featured (large) article, use class="news-card featured". News is displayed in the order written in HTML — newest first.

📷 Upload Photos

How to add real photos:
1. Upload your photos to your server's /images/ folder.
2. Find the GALLERY_ITEMS array in the JavaScript section.
3. For each item, change img: null to img: '/images/your-photo.jpg'.
4. The gallery items will then display your actual photos instead of placeholder icons.

For the hero section: Replace the SVG boxer illustration with an <img src="/images/lekeisha-hero.jpg" alt="Lekeisha Pergoliti"> tag inside .athlete-silhouette.

Contact form backend (contact.php): Create a PHP file that validates POST data, applies rate limiting by IP, sanitises all inputs, and sends email via PHPMailer. Never expose API keys in frontend code. Use environment variables for SMTP credentials.
Hosting recommendation: SiteGround or WP Engine (Australia region) for Australian SEO advantage. Enable Cloudflare CDN for performance and DDoS protection. Use Let's Encrypt for free SSL.
